Privacy Policy for Grade Agent

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Grade Agent ("the App," "we," "us") is operated by Dalton Shirley, doing business as Grade Agent. Grade Agent helps you scan and grade trading cards. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect details you provide such as username, email address, and authentication credentials.

Scan and usage data. We record scan activity (including scan counts for freemium limits), app feature use, and related technical logs needed to run grading, paywall, profile, and leaderboard features.

Profile and social features. If you use profiles or the leaderboard, information you choose to show (such as username and Pro status) may be visible to other users.

Purchases. Subscriptions are processed by Apple, Google, and/or RevenueCat. We receive entitlement status (for example, whether your account is Pro). We do not store your full payment card number.

Device and diagnostics. We may collect device type, OS version, app version, crash and performance data, and approximate network information to keep the App reliable.

How we use information

We use this information to provide and improve Grade Agent, enforce free-scan limits, deliver Pro features, show Pro status where designed (for example profile or leaderboard badges), process restores and subscription management, prevent abuse, and respond to support requests.

How we share information

We share data with service providers who help us operate the App, including hosting, analytics, crash reporting, and subscription billing (such as RevenueCat and the Apple App Store or Google Play). We may disclose information if required by law or to protect users and the App. We do not sell your personal information.

Data retention

We keep account and usage data while your account is active and as needed for legitimate business, legal, and security purposes. You can request deletion as described below.

Your choices

You can update profile details in the App, manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple or Google account settings (or in-app Manage/Restore where available), and contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of your account data where applicable law allows.

Children's privacy

Grade Agent is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

International users

If you use the App from outside the country where our servers or providers operate, your information may be processed in other countries that may have different data-protection rules.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the revised version with a new "Last updated" date.
