Common topics

Free scans and Pro. Free accounts get 10 scans. Pro unlocks unlimited scans, the full sub-grade breakdown, and AI observations. Prices shown in the App are $6.99 per month or $49.99 per year (with a 7-day free trial on the yearly plan where available).

Restore purchases. Open Settings in the App and use Restore if you reinstalled or switched devices. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID or Google Play account.

Manage or cancel a subscription. Use Manage in Settings, or cancel in your device's subscription settings (Apple ID subscriptions or Google Play subscriptions). Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.

Sign out. Use Sign out in Settings.

Scan or grading issues. Tell us your username, device type, OS version, and what you expected versus what happened. A screenshot helps.

Account deletion. Email Hordefather23@outlook.com from the address on your account and ask to delete your account. We will confirm when it is done.
